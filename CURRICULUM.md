## Final capstone

Build a Circom + snarkjs + Solidity demonstration proving that a privately held collateral amount A and haircut H in basis points satisfy a public threshold T: floor(A × (10000 − H) / 10000) ≥ T. Range-constrain A and H; bind T as a public signal; document field arithmetic pitfalls; test valid and invalid witnesses; verify the Groth16 proof on-chain. Educational only, not production safe.
